// LOADOUT

Everything an office needs, in one small app

Made for everyday work, not for the internet. Here is what your staff get.

Chat and groups

One-to-one and group chats with reactions, replies, @mentions, edit and delete, pinned and starred messages, voice notes, stickers and "seen" ticks. Messages to someone who is offline wait and arrive when they are back.

Files of any size

Drag files or whole folders into a chat — no size limit. Big files travel over several connections at once and resume if interrupted. Share a folder that stays in sync by itself.

Calls and meetings

Voice and video calls, group meetings for up to 12 people, screen sharing in full resolution, raise hand, meeting chat and recording — saved on your own PC, never anywhere else.

Remote help and remote control

See a colleague's screen and, if they press Allow, use their mouse and keyboard to fix things — like AnyDesk, but only on your network. They stay in charge: an orange frame, a Stop bar, and their own mouse always wins. No passwords, no unattended access.

PDF tools

PDFs open in LANX: read, find words, print; erase, highlight, draw, text, stamps and your signature; merge, split, compress, pages to pictures, page numbers, watermark, header and footer, fill forms.

Organiser

Post notices everyone must confirm, polls, tasks with due dates, approvals, shared checklists and meeting invites — right inside a chat. The Organiser shows what is waiting for you from every chat.

Office tools

Emergency alert with "I'm safe" replies, a hold-to-talk intercom, a reception desk that tells a colleague their visitor is here, birthdays, kudos badges, out-of-office replies and a staff directory.

IT help desk

"Get IT help" sends a ticket with your PC's details and a picture of your screen to your IT person, who can press See their screen and mark it resolved. Every ticket is listed in the Organiser.

Who's in, end-of-day reports

See who is in the office, working from home, away or offline today. Staff send a short end-of-day report (done, pending, stuck) to their manager, who marks it seen.

Private by design

End-to-end encryption, a PIN lock, secret chats that disappear, hidden chats, and a security code to verify a colleague. Your data lives in one file on your PC and goes nowhere else.

Backups

A copy of your chats every day, restore to any of the last seven, and password-protected backup files for a USB drive or a new computer — optionally mirrored to a network folder.

Updates over the network

Install a new version on one PC and LANX offers it to the colleagues you choose. Only signed LANX releases are accepted, and nothing installs without a click.

Admin, exports, apps

One PC can be the LANX admin and share company apps, message clean-up and a backup folder with everyone. Export any chat or the staff directory to Excel or PDF.

Games, music, memes

Hidden corners for the lunch break: type /game for 49 games with colleagues (chess, Ludo, cards, party games, air hockey…) on 3D boards with sound, /music for a music player, /meme for memes and GIFs.

// HOW IT WORKS

Three steps. No IT project.

  1. 01

    Install LANX on each PC

    Download the installer and run it. Type your name and you're in. Takes about a minute per computer.

  2. 02

    Be on the same office network

    Wi-Fi or cable, it doesn't matter. The internet can be slow or off — LANX never uses it.

  3. 03

    Colleagues appear by themselves

    Every PC announces itself and introduces the others. Pick a name on the left and start chatting or sending files.

Someone doesn't show up? Office networks are sometimes split into parts, or a Wi-Fi has "client isolation" on. Use Peers → Connect by IP once (they can see their address in Settings → Network), and LANX remembers them.

// DEPLOYMENT

Installing on Windows

One click: open the file, say Yes, and LANX installs and opens by itself.

  1. Download the installer with the button above and open it from your Downloads folder.
  2. A blue box "Windows protected your PC" may appear. That is Windows SmartScreen being careful with any new program downloaded from the internet. Click More info, then Run anyway. Prefer not to see it at all? Right-click the downloaded file → Properties → tick Unblock → OK, then open it. Files copied over a USB drive or a shared folder never show this box.
  3. Windows asks "Do you want to allow this app to make changes to your device?" → Yes. That is the only click: LANX installs with a progress bar (no questions), lets itself through the Windows Firewall so there are no pop-ups later, and opens.
  4. LANX opens. Type your name → Launch LANX. A short animated guide shows you around (find it again under Settings → Help).

Requirements

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
  • About 30 MB of disk space
  • All PCs on the same office network
  • Microsoft Edge WebView2 (already part of Windows; the installer adds it if missing)
  • Linux: Ubuntu 22.04+, Debian 12+, Mint 21+ (.deb) or any distribution (AppImage)

Upgrading

Just run the new installer. It replaces the old copy and keeps every chat and file. Or install it on one PC and let LANX offer it to the others.

Where is my data?

In %APPDATA%\LANX on each PC, and nowhere else. Received files land in Downloads.

// FAQ

Questions people ask

Does LANX need the internet?

No. It only uses your office network (Wi-Fi or cable). It keeps working when the internet is down, and nothing you write or send ever leaves the building.

Is there a server, a login or a monthly fee?

None of the three. Each PC talks directly to the others. You install it, type your name, and that's it.

Can people at home or in another branch use it?

Only if their PC is on the same network — for example through your company's VPN. LANX is made for the people inside one office; it is not an internet messenger.

Is it safe? Can an admin read my messages?

Messages and files are encrypted end-to-end between the two PCs and stored only on those PCs. The LANX admin can share office settings and see who needs an update, but can never read anyone's chats.

Why does Windows show "Windows protected your PC"?

Because LANX is a small program without a paid code-signing certificate, Windows is careful the first time you run a downloaded copy. Click More info → Run anyway, or right-click the file → Properties → Unblock. Copies that colleagues receive through LANX itself never show the box.

Can someone take control of my PC without me knowing?

No. Remote control only starts after you press Allow on your own PC, every single time. An orange frame and a Stop bar stay on your screen the whole time, your own mouse always wins, and there is no password or unattended mode at all.

How do updates work?

Download and run the new installer — chats are kept. Or install it on one PC, open Settings → Updates, tick the colleagues who should get it and press Send update. LANX only accepts releases carrying the LANX signature.

Is it open source?

Yes — the code is on GitHub. It is built with Tauri (Rust) and React.
